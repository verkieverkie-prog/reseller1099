Where to download your CSV on each platform

Whatnot

whatnot.com -> Seller Menu -> Finances -> Statements & Reports -> Weekly Orders Report (CSV). Supports both Title Case and UPPER_SNAKE_CASE exports and separates giveaways from refunds.

Poshmark

Web: Profile -> Order Activity -> Sales Report -> Email Report. Automatically skips Poshmark's 12 preamble lines and infers the hidden 20% / $2.95 Poshmark fee.

Mercari

Mercari.com -> Account -> Seller Dashboard -> Sales Report (or Yearly Reports). Recovers unescaped inch-marks in Item Titles and ignores buyer-paid fees.

Etsy

Shop Manager -> Finances -> Monthly statements -> Generate CSV. Subtracts buyer sales tax from Sale rows, excludes Deposit transfers, and keeps duplicate $0.20 listing fees.
